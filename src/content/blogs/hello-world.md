Welcome! I've been wanting to start a blog for years, and today I'm finally doing it.

## Why Start a Blog?

I've learned so much from other developers' blogs over the years. It's time to give back.

Writing also forces me to **solidify my understanding** of topics. When you can explain something clearly, you truly understand it.

## What to Expect

I'll be writing about:

- **Web development** — React, TypeScript, Node.js, and more
- **Career growth** — lessons learned, mistakes made
- **Side projects** — building in public
- **Tools & workflows** — what's working for me

## A Promise to Myself

I'll publish at least twice a month. No matter how short, something is better than nothing.

Thanks for reading. Let's build something great together! 🚀
