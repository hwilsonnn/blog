How to Win Friends and Influence People - Dale Carnegie
* Been reading bits of pieces of this throughout the week, the crux of it is just be nice to people (obviously!). People are by default interested in themselves, so I've found the following rules from the '[Six Ways to Make People Like You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_to_Win_Friends_and_Influence_People#Six_Ways_to_Make_People_Like_You)' section to be quite true:
    1. Become genuinely interested in other people.
    2. Smile.
    3. Remember that a person's name is, to that person, the sweetest and most important sound in any language.
    4. Be a good listener. Encourage others to talk about themselves.
    5. Talk in terms of the other person's interest.
    6. Make the other person feel important – and do it sincerely.
* This has been quite useful during my first few weeks at work in meeting new people, but it's even valuable outside of work too.
